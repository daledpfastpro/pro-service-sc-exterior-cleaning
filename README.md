**House washing** helps refresh exterior walls by addressing dirt and environmental buildup. Selecting the appropriate cleaning technique is important to protect siding and other exterior materials.

**Soft washing** uses lower water pressure and suitable cleaning solutions for compatible surfaces. This approach can be useful for exterior areas that require a gentler cleaning method.

**Roof cleaning** helps address visible staining and organic growth on appropriate roofing materials. The cleaning method should always be selected according to the roof type and its care requirements.
